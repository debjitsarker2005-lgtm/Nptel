2. Ans: Option 1 and 2 
  Setting a threshold for the decrease in sum of squared errors (SSE) prevents 
insignificant splits, thereby limiting tree growth.   
The threshold controls model complexity, so it can improve or worsen predictive 
performance depending on the chosen value.  
The method is computationally feasible and is commonly used as a stopping 
criterion. 
 
3. Ans: Option 2 and 3 
As a decision tree continues to grow: 
Bias decreases → A deeper tree is more flexible and can fit the training data 
better.  
Variance increases → The deeper tree becomes more sensitive to the particular 
training data and may overfit.  
So, the bias–variance trade-off is: 
Tree depth ↑ → Bias ↓, Variance ↑
