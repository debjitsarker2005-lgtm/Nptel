Although both linear SVM and the Perceptron can produce a linear decision boundary for linearly separable data, they optimize different objectives:
Perceptron: Finds any separating hyperplane that correctly classifies the training data. 
SVM: Finds the separating hyperplane that maximizes the margin between the two classes. 
Therefore, even with the same linearly separable training data, they do not necessarily produce the same classifier.
